Speakers & headphones

Each button plays a short tone. Left should only come from the left side, right only from the right.

The sweep rises from 40 Hz to 16,000 Hz. Rattling or buzzing at low pitches can mean a damaged speaker. Most adults stop hearing the tone somewhere above 12,000 Hz.
